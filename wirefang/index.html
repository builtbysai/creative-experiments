Creative Experiments · № 04

Wirefang: K-9 / Zero

A 2019 line drawing of a dog, traced by code and grown back as something new. Nothing below is a pasted image — the outline was recovered from the original pixel by pixel, and everything you see is drawn by your browser.

Study I

The outline, drawn by code

127 strokes recovered from the 2019 original: threshold the ink, thin it to a one-pixel skeleton, trace every branch. Press replay and watch the pen retrace the dog.

127 strokes · 465 points
Study II

The generative recreation

The recovered outline becomes a cage. Inside the silhouette, 3,200 wire-fur strokes grow along a flow field — no two coats alike. Move your cursor like a lantern across the fur. The eye keeps its ember.

Reference

The 2019 originals

Kept as found, renamed. One drawing, six colorings — the lineage this piece continues.
